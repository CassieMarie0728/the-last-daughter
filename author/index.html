Cassandra Crossno is the author of The Last Daughter of the First Fyre: Volume One — The Embercrest Awakening, set in the world of Aethrakar.

At the heart of this story is a woman whose power does not cancel her pain—and whose usefulness to other people does not give them ownership of her.

This is a world of dangerous magic, fractured history, difficult loyalties, and the stubborn work of choosing who you are when everyone else has an answer ready.
