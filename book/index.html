The Embercrest Awakening

Volume One of The Last Daughter of the First Fyre

She came home.
Something answered.

Eight years ago, Madisyn Fyre’s awakening left her with a permanent injury, living fyre, and a village that never forgot. Now a monster hunter, she has learned to survive on her own terms.

Then the ash begins falling uphill.

Thornhearth’s protections are failing. Something is driving corruption toward Embercrest. The woman who trained her has kept dangerous secrets, and the authorities arriving to protect the village have their own plans for Madisyn’s power.

To find answers, she must follow a broken history through forbidden records and uneasy alliances—while a voice inside her fyre calls her by a name she refuses to accept.

Saving a world should not mean surrendering yourself.
