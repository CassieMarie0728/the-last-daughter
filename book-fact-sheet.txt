THE LAST DAUGHTER OF THE FIRST FYRE
Volume One: The Embercrest Awakening
Author: Cassandra Crossno
Genre: Dark fantasy
Language: English

SHORT DESCRIPTION
When warm ash begins falling uphill, monster hunter Madisyn Fyre returns to a village that remembers her worst day. As Embercrest's protections fail, her living fyre draws the attention of powers determined to decide what she owes the world. A dark fantasy of buried history, dangerous trust, and the right to refuse.
