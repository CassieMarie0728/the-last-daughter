A world built on buried truths.

An illustrated companion to the world. Places beyond Volume One appear on this map.

01 / Where the wound begins

Thornhearth

Slate roofs, black stone walls, a creek, and a village with a long memory. Beneath the lower slopes of Embercrest, Madisyn’s homecoming begins with warm ash drifting toward the mountain.

02 / The mountain remembers

Embercrest

Failing protections. Bitter springs. Corruption moving against gravity. Beneath the mountain, old bindings connect the present crisis to a history that refuses to stay buried.

03 / A refuge of forbidden knowledge

Corwin’s Observatory

The climb is difficult; the answers are harder. Corwin’s observatory holds records, instruments, and evidence that the old protections are part of something larger.

04 / Trust what you can prove

The Veilwoods

Along the hidden routes, voices, memories, and altered records unsettle the travelers. The forest holds evidence, but that does not make it an infallible witness.

What holds the world together?

Living fyre

Madisyn’s fyre behaves less like a tool than an intelligence with opinions of its own. Its responses raise questions; they do not settle who—or what—is speaking.

The old bindings

The protections around Embercrest form a connected network. A failure in one place can move the burden somewhere else.

Competing powers

The Argent Registry and the Pale Brand promise order. The Hidden Hearth shelters people beyond their reach. Safety means different things depending on who gets to define it.
